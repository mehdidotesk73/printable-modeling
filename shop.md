# Shop

Clearances depend on the printer, so measured values here beat anything from the internet.

## Printer
- Model: Creality Ender-3 V3 SE (direct-drive extruder, auto bed levelling)
- Nozzle: 0.4 mm
- Bed / build volume: 220 x 220 x 250 mm (published spec). Plan pieces to ≤ ~210 mm to leave margin.

## Materials
- PLA (main). Nozzle ~215 °C, bed 65 °C first layer then 60 °C (**to verify** on the printer).

## Slicer
- Creality slicer. Layer height: _to check_ (0.2 mm assumed until confirmed).

## Fit experience
- Fits have so far needed lots of trial and error, so the clearance test (case 000) comes first.
